Fixed price · 1 to 2 weeks

Why work with me

1

You come first

I measure my work by one thing: are you happy with the result? I start from your problem, not from the technology, and you see progress every week, not a big surprise at the end.

2

Good practices, good products

Tests first, small releases, and a simple design that stays easy to change. These habits from Extreme Programming are what turn software into a product you can rely on. With or without AI, a bad design only grows faster.

3

Clear communication

Early in my career I read Kent Beck's Extreme Programming Explained, and one idea stayed with me: communication is the key to success. You get plain answers and a short update whenever you need it.

4

I figure things out

Nobody has every answer, and I won't pretend to. What I bring is a way to find them together: we ask the right questions, try ideas in baby steps, and amplify what the whole team can do.
